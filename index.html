About Us

Andrew Tree Felling Services is a trusted and professional tree service company with over 15 years of hands-on experience serving the entire Pretoria area.

We are fully insured and covered for accidental property damage and personal injury, including full Workman’s Compensation for our staff, giving our clients total peace of mind.

Our team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including emergency call-outs for fallen trees, storm damage, and dangerous tree removals.
